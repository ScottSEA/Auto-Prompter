# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Presenters, speakers, educators, and content creators who read a prepared script while looking
toward a camera or audience. They use the app at arm's length or farther away, often under time
pressure and variable ambient light, and need the script to remain readable without constant touch
interaction.

## Product Purpose

Auto-Prompter keeps a script aligned with the speaker's spoken position, supports dependable manual
and hardware-remote control, and eventually records clean camera video while the prompt remains
visible. Success means the presenter can concentrate on delivery rather than scrolling, recover
immediately when speech following is uncertain, and trust that scripts, settings, recordings, and
the permanent unlock remain local-first and dependable.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, professional. The interface should feel quiet and dependable during performance,
with direct language and no decorative competition with the script.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a cluttered developer diagnostics screen. Avoid dense control-room chrome, exposed
implementation terminology, equal-weight panels, gratuitous metrics, flashy creator-app styling,
or interactions that make the presenter manage the system instead of reading.

## Design Principles

1. The script is the product surface; controls recede until needed.
2. Preserve presenter confidence through predictable state, explicit recovery, and no surprise
   movement.
3. Make every primary action operable by touch, keyboard, and presentation remote.
4. Degrade honestly when speech, camera, network, storage, or platform capabilities are unavailable.
5. Keep user content local-first and explain every external transfer before it occurs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA where applicable. Prompt text and controls require strong contrast, generous target
sizes, scalable type, meaningful screen-reader labels, and no color-only state communication.
Support keyboard and presentation-remote operation, reduced-motion behavior, RTL/localized text,
and distance reading without requiring precise touch input.
